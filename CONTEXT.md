# Cyprus dating

Dating for people whose primary home is in the Republic of Cyprus.

## Language

**Resident**:
A person whose primary home is in the Operating area. Settled expats count. A holiday or short-stay visitor does not.
_Avoid_: User (when you mean this audience rule), local (ambiguous), citizen

**Visitor**:
A person who is in the Operating area but whose primary home is not. Holiday, business trip, and a short digital-nomad stay are Visitors.
_Avoid_: Tourist (too narrow), guest

**Operating area**:
The Republic of Cyprus. Not Northern Cyprus, and not matching across the Green Line, in v1.
_Avoid_: Cyprus (when you mean the whole island), the south (as the official name)

**Pool**:
The single set of Residents who can discover each other. There is one Pool, not one per Launch language.
_Avoid_: Market, feed (as the set of people), silo

**Launch language**:
One of English, Ukrainian, Russian, Romanian, or Bulgarian. The languages the product speaks in v1.
_Avoid_: Locale (when you mean a human language), Greek (not a Launch language)

**Profile**:
The photos, first name, age, city, languages spoken, and short bio a Resident shows to others.
_Avoid_: Account (the login), bio (the whole Profile)

**Interest**:
A one-way expression that a Resident wants to Match with another Resident they can see.
_Avoid_: Like, swipe, crush (as the domain noun)

**Match**:
The state after two Residents have Interest in each other, which opens chat.
_Avoid_: Connection, pair, conversation (the Match is the state; chat is what it opens)

**Photo verification**:
A check that the person in the Profile photos is the person holding the phone. Unverified Profiles stay in the Pool and are marked.
_Avoid_: KYC, ID check, verification (unqualified)
